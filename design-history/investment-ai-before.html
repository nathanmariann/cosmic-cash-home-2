Traditional vs Roth accounts

Traditional contributions may be deductible; withdrawals are generally taxable. Roth contributions are after tax; qualified withdrawals can be tax-free. Eligibility and rules vary.

Starting age depends on birth year and account type. Traditional retirement accounts generally require distributions; Roth IRAs generally do not during the owner's lifetime. Verify your applicable deadline and amount with the IRS.
